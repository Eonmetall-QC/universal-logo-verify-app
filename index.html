Logo Verify

Point at the logo and take a photo

Hold the device parallel to the logo and let it fill most of the frame. Tilt slightly to remove glare.

  1. Reading the ink
  2. Finding the logo
  3. Correcting angle and perspective
  4. Comparing with the master

The logo is found automatically. If the photo shows more than one logo, drag a box around the one to check. Any angle works, even sideways or upside down. For a sharp photo, tap the logo on the camera screen to focus, then hold still.
